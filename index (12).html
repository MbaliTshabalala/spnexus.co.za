About

A firm built around the month, not the emergency.

SP Nexus exists for businesses that need reliable operational and administrative support — whether they are establishing, growing or refining how the work gets done.

Who we work with

Businesses across different stages and markets that need practical support covering administration, documentation, operations, strategy, customer experience, performance tracking, specialist interventions, Business Revival, events, property and logistics.

We work with organisations looking to establish, operate, improve or expand within South Africa and across Africa.

SP Nexus (Pty) Ltd is a registered South African company based in Pretoria, Gauteng. We do not claim volumes or turnaround times we cannot stand behind. The work is the package.

Mission

To give each client a clear monthly view of administration, documents, finances where included, and priorities — and the capacity to act on it.

Services

What sits in the work

Core capabilities that appear across packages and specialist engagements. Scope is always written down.

Administrative support

Defined monthly capacity for operational work that otherwise sits with the owner.

Business documentation

A monthly document cadence so letters, records and operating documents stay current.

Email support

Ongoing support on every package, with priority handling on Elevate and Bespoke.

Priority action review

A monthly review of what must be done next so the work of the month is chosen on purpose.

Business Health Score

A scored view of how the business is running, with a monthly report and trend insight on higher packages.

Bookkeeping capture

Capture of bookkeeping information and monthly summaries on Elevate and Bespoke.

Strategy sessions

Quarterly sessions on Bespoke to review recommendations and priorities.

Dedicated consultant

A named consultant on Bespoke accountable for the relationship and quality of work.
